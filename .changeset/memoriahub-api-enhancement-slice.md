Add the `enhancement` slice to `@marinoscar/memoriahub-api` (private; `@marinoscar/memoriahub-api/enhancement` and `.../enhancement/testing`): the AI Picture Enhancer (`MediaEnhancementService`: single and bulk start, list, poll, keep-both, replace, discard, batch inspection and cancel), the server-only `picture_enhancement` job (every model call through `MEMORIAHUB_AI`; type string, profile and the absent node result pair unchanged), the `picture_enhancement_purge` job and its enqueue-only hourly cron, the three controllers over `@MemoriahubAuth` as exported classes, and `describeEnhancementSliceConformance`. Host hooks replace what stayed in the web app: `ENHANCEMENT_IMAGE_TOOLS` (sharp and ExifTool stay there), `ENHANCEMENT_KEY_STATUS` and `ENHANCEMENT_STORAGE_ORG`, plus the `rateLimitMaxHits` and `housekeepingEnabled` options of `EnhancementModule.forRoot`. `MemoriahubJob` gains an optional `rateLimitHits` (the web app's jobs binding fills it) so a handler can mirror the queue's retry decision. The web app binds it in `apps/api/src/enhancement`. Routes, job types and settings are unchanged; OpenAPI is byte-identical.
