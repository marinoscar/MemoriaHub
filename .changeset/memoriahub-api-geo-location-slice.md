Move the geo half into the `location` slice of `@marinoscar/memoriahub-api` (private; `@marinoscar/memoriahub-api/location`): the offline (GeoNames), Nominatim and Google reverse providers, `GeoLocationService`, `DelegatingGeoLocationProvider` (bound to `GEO_LOCATION_PROVIDER` by `LocationModule.forRoot()`), `ForwardGeocodeService`, `GeoGoogleCredentialResolver`, `GeoSettingsService` (the Google key through the secrets port; never returned or logged), `GeocodeBackfillService`, the `geocode` job (`GeocodeHandler` with `GoogleGeocodeKeyBroker`; type string, 60 second profile, three attempts, node eligibility and per-job key delivery unchanged; a provider quota signal is now a `MemoriahubRateLimitError` on the `geocode` throttle bucket) and the `GeoSettingsController`, `GeocodeAdminController` and `GeocodeMediaController` classes over `@MemoriahubAuth`. `LocationInferenceModule.forRoot` no longer takes `geoProviderImports`. `local-reverse-geocoder` is an optional peer of the slice, loaded on first use. The web app binds the media hooks to these classes and mounts the controllers; routes, job types and settings are unchanged and the OpenAPI document is byte-identical.
