Add the `location` slice to `@marinoscar/memoriahub-api` (private; `@marinoscar/memoriahub-api/location` and `.../location/testing`), location grouping and location inference. `LocationGroupResolverService` (the one writer of canonical place names: alias beats radius beats raw), the `location_group_rebuild` job and its enqueue helper, `LocationGroupsService`, location inference (`LocationInferenceService`, the `location_inference` job, the sweep backfill, `LocationSuggestionService`, the upload fan-out registration, `LocationInferenceModule.forRoot({ geoProviderImports })`), the geo provider contract (`GEO_LOCATION_PROVIDER`, `applyLocation`, `geoResultToMediaColumns`), the shared `haversineKm` and `whereNear`, `fold`/`suggestionKey`, and `describeLocationSliceConformance`. The media slice gains `enqueueEnrichmentJob` and `resolveMediaItemJob` (the enrichment queue's subject convention over the jobs port). Routes, job type strings and settings are unchanged; the controllers, the geo providers and the `geocode` job stay in the web app for now. The jobs port's `MemoriahubJob` gains an optional `reason`; the media slice gains `featureSkipReason` and `resolveCircleJob`.
